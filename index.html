01 · Intake

Pulled in around the cylinder

Room air enters through vertical slots in the 3D-printed upper housing. The first PMS7003M laser sensor samples this incoming air before it reaches the filter.

02 · Filter

Through the HEPA cartridge

A cylindrical HEPA cartridge sits inside the housing. A printed TPU lip seal closes off the top of the cartridge, held in place by a center column underneath it. The seal only works one way round, so its orientation is called out in the hardware notes.

03 · Measure

A second sensor checks the result

Filtered air passes a second PMS7003M. A BME680 adds temperature, humidity, pressure and a VOC trend. Both particulate sensors are matched with gain and offset before any efficiency is shown.

Try it: switch off Seal support installed to see the bypass leak that once dragged a test to 57%.

04 · Blower base

Down into the electronics base

Clean air drops into a separate base where a Noctua NF-F12 iPPC-3000 PWM fan moves it. Fan speed follows particulate load, with spike detection, ramp-up and settling, and a manual override in the UI.

05 · Exhaust

Out through the base slots

Air leaves through ventilation slots around the base. Meanwhile the ESP32-C6 serves a six-tab dashboard from LittleFS over local Wi-Fi: no cloud account and no app to install.

Tap a tab to open it in the dashboard demo below.

Air path

The filter wasn't the problem

A test came back at 57%. Rather than blame the HEPA media, I traced the air path and found the center column that supports the top TPU seal was missing. Without it the seal could not hold against the cartridge, so unfiltered air slipped past the top of the filter and straight to the post-filter sensor.

Sealed-chamber test57%→96.6%
RF noise

Wi-Fi made fake smoke

Transmit bursts coupled into the sensor electronics and showed up as false PM spikes. Fixed with local bulk and ceramic decoupling, ferrite chokes on sensor cables, star grounding and more antenna separation.

Calibration

Two sensors, one truth

Identical sensors disagreed by 35%. A two-parameter gain/offset match with validation guards brought the residual to −1.7% on that build.

Post-sensor error+35%→−1.7%
